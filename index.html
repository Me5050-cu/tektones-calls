Tektones call sheet

Five churches a week each. Tap your name to see yours.

What to say
Opening

"Hi, my name's [name]. I'm a student intern working with a company called Tektones. Is the person who runs your kids ministry around?"

What it is

"We make building sets of Bible stories. The one I'm calling about is the Nativity — 879 pieces, ten figures, and it comes with a family devotional built to take a family through Advent a few pieces a night."

The two questions

"Two quick things. Is that something your kids ministry would even be interested in this Christmas? And if you did want a few — for the kids wing, or as Christmas gifts for your preschool teachers or volunteers — what price per set would you actually be able to work with?"

Close

"That helps a lot, thank you. What's the best email for you? I'll send a photo and the details today, and I'll follow up next week once I have the final pricing."

Voicemail, 15 seconds

"Hi, this is [name]. I'm a student intern with Tektones — we make Bible-story building sets. I've got a free Advent lesson I'd like to send your kids ministry, no strings. My number is [your number]. I'll try again Thursday morning."

Write their number in the price box on the card, even if it's low. What churches say they'd pay is the whole point of this week.

If they ask whether you make anything on it: no. It's a school internship, you're not paid and you don't take a cut. Tektones is a real company that sells the sets. Don't call it a fundraiser or a donation, and never say "LEGO" — say building set.

If they push back

"Just email me."

"Happy to, I'll send it today. Can I call you back Tuesday once you've had a look?" Email is the receipt, never the pitch.

"We don't bring in outside vendors."

"Understood. Can I still send the free Advent lesson? No strings, use it or don't."

"There's no budget for that."

"That's the good part — a lobby table costs the church nothing. Money goes the other direction, $10 a set back to your kids ministry."

"I'd have to ask the pastor."

"Of course. Want me to send one page you can forward? I'll write it so you don't have to explain it."

"Are you making money on this?"

"No. I'm a student intern — I don't get paid for this and I don't take a commission. Tektones is a real company and they do sell the sets, but for me this is a school project."

"We're in the middle of shoeboxes."

"I know, collection week is the 16th through the 23rd. That's why I'm calling now instead of November."

"Can I see one first?"

"Yes. I can drop a built one at the office this week and you can keep it in the kids wing through Advent."

"How much is it?"

"Pricing goes live this week. What's the best number to text you the second it does?" Then actually text them.
